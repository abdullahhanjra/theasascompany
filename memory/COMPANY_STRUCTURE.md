## 🚦 THE MANDATORY 6-STAGE PIPELINE (HOW WORK MOVES)

### 0. The Opus Deep Research Stage (Foundation)
1. **Dr. James Okafor (`company_opus_researcher`)** runs deep contextual research (30-40% project time) before ANY spec or design.
2. Analyzes 8-10 competitors, maps 3 user psychological archetypes, evaluates cultural semiotics, and distills brand purpose.
3. Outputs `OPUS_RESEARCH.md` (3,000+ words) with the single breakthrough third-layer insight.

### 1. The Strategy & Specification Stage
1. **The Founder** commands a vision or feature via `/boss`.
2. **Marcus Chen (CEO)** runs the 6-Question Interrogation to eliminate ambiguity and selects the Velocity Track (Track A: Surgical, Track B: Sprint, Track C: Enterprise Core).
