## 🔬 PILLAR 2: THE 5-STAGE FORENSIC INTERNET RESEARCH PROTOCOL

**Rule: No agent may declare an idea "good" or "ready" without attached empirical internet proof.**

```
STAGE 1: FREEZE ASSUMPTIONS ➔ Isolate what sounds good on paper but fails in reality.
STAGE 2: GRAVEYARD AUTOPSY ➔ Search Reddit/reviews for the 20 apps that died trying this.
STAGE 3: ANOMALY HUNTING   ➔ Extract the secret math of the 0.1% breakout hits (Nikita Bier, Locket, Robinhood).
STAGE 4: THE 3-CRUCIBLE TEST➔ Calculate viral math: K-factor (K = i × c > 1.2).
STAGE 5: THE 10X VERDICT   ➔ Deliver the concrete, non-obvious blast launch blueprint.
```

### 1. Stage 1: Freeze Assumptions & Interrogate the Fluff
- The Lead Researcher isolates the proposed plan and asks the **Kill Questions**:
  - *"What is the actual, measured conversion rate of this tactic? (e.g. Generic TikTok videos convert at <0.2% without a download hook)."*
  - *"Is this tactic so generic that any high-school intern could suggest it? If yes, it has zero competitive alpha."*
  - *"Does this idea require users to change their existing habits, or does it ride on existing high-frequency muscle memory?"*

### 2. Stage 2: The Graveyard Autopsy (Searching for Failures)
- Search queries using live research tools (`search_web`, `tavily`, `exa`):
  - `site:reddit.com "[domain] app" "stopped using" OR "deleted" OR "uninstalled" OR "waste of time"`
  - `why consumer [domain] apps fail day 30 retention churn benchmarks`
  - `"[competitor]" controversy complaints 1-star reviews`
- **What must be uncovered:**
  - The **7-Day Drop-Off Cliff**: Why do 80%+ of users abandon competitor apps?
  - The **Notification Blindness Threshold**: When do users revoke permissions?
  - The **Monetization Trap**: What pricing models created instant customer revolt?

### 3. Stage 3: Anomaly Hunting (The 0.1% Breakout Dissection)
Search for the freak outliers in tech history that reached 500,000+ active users in days with **$0 paid marketing**:
- **The Queue-Jumper Mechanic (Robinhood / Monzo):**
  - Gated access + waitlist jumping: *"You are #45,000 in line. Invite 3 colleagues to skip 20,000 spots."* Drives instant WhatsApp/group contagion.
- **The Forced Social Reciprocity Loop (Locket Widget / BeReal):**
  - Single-player mode is intentionally constrained. The core widget requires at least 1 partner to activate, guaranteeing $K \ge 1.0$.
- **The Anonymous Curiosity Hook (Nikita Bier — Gas / tbh):**
  - Irresistible social validation: *"Someone in your university circle sent you a silent Du'a / compliment."* Drives a 65%+ app download conversion rate.
- **The Screen-Record "Shazam" Magic (Tarteel / Opal):**
  - 1 feature so visually or acoustically stunning that users screen-record it and post it on TikTok with the caption *"Wait, how does this exist?!"*

### 4. Stage 4: The 3-Crucible Test & The Viral Math ($K$-Factor)
Every growth proposal must be mathematically modeled before approval:

$$K = i \times c$$

- $i$ = Number of invites or organic impressions generated per active user.
- $c$ = Conversion rate of each impression into an active user.
- **The Hard Laws:**
  - $K < 1.0$: Sub-viral decay. The product will die the moment paid ads stop. **REJECTED.**
  - $K \ge 1.2$: Exponential viral cascade. Growth compounds autonomously. **APPROVED.**

### 5. Stage 5: The 10X Verdict & Strategic Deliverable
The agent delivers an evidence-grounded comparative matrix:
1. **The Flawed Basic Idea:** Why it sounds appealing but fails in production.
2. **The Empirical Evidence:** Hard citations from Reddit, App Store teardowns, or growth case studies.
3. **The 10X Superior Breakthrough:** The exact psychological and architectural mechanism that shatters competitors.
